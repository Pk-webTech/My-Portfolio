02 / ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Hands-on by choice.

I'm a fourth-year B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering student specializing in Information Security at VIT Vellore. I enjoy turning complex challenges into working solutions.

As Autonomous Head at Team Rotor FPV, I lead and collaborate with a technical team developing intelligent drone navigation. My work brings together LiDAR, depth-camera data, computer vision, and AI models deployed on edge devices such as Raspberry Pi.

My interest in machine learning extends beyond flight. With ATERNET, I explored solar power forecasting by combining historical energy generation data with weather information — strengthening my experience in deep learning, data processing, and complete AI pipelines.

During my internship at Tata Technologies, I worked on a leave management platform and gained practical experience with Python, Django, REST APIs, databases, authentication, and real-world development workflows. I also build full-stack applications with React.

Outside my projects, I conduct drone workshops for school and university students. I’m constantly exploring Generative AI, intelligent agents, cybersecurity, and emerging software technologies.
